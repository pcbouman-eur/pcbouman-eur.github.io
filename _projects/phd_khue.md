I am the daily supervisor of [Khuê Nguyễn](https://www.eur.nl/people/hoang-thi-khue-nguyen)

## Abstract of the Project Proposal

> The coming years, more construction works will take place on the railway networks. On days with
> construction works, the timetable needs to be adjusted. At this moment, this process starts several
> months before the day of operation and is finished around 4 weeks before the day of operation.
> The process starts with adjusting the basic hour pattern to an alternative hour pattern.
> This alternative hour pattern is a cyclic timetable of 1 hour and can be repeated throughout the day.
> Preferably, it is created such that the impact of the construction work on the quality of service and
> the regular operations is limited as much as possible. There is limited literature to construct such
> an alternative hour pattern. In our research project, we want to develop new models and algorithms
> for constructing these alternative hour patterns.
